Questions

Does the guest need an app?+

No. A phone is enough: a tap for NFC or the camera for the QR code. The page opens in the browser.

Which package should I choose?+

If you only need Google reviews, Basic. If you want your venue’s own page with messages, contacts and opening hours, Standard. For tables, waiters, bookings and several venues, Business. For restaurants that want guests to experience the menu, Signature. Not sure? Get in touch and we will suggest one.

Does it work on every phone?+

NFC works on almost all recent phones, iPhone and Android. For the rest, there is the QR code on the same card.

Is the website really on the chip?+

The chip stores the address of your page, and the page itself lives on our server. That is why we can change it whenever needed while the card stays the same. If a card is damaged, a new one gets the same address and everything works as before.

Is it in line with Google’s rules?+

Yes. Every guest sees the same options, we never reward anyone for reviews and we never choose who may write one. The card just shortens the way to Google’s form, and the message to you is an extra option, not a replacement.

Why a monthly subscription?+

Basic has no subscription: you pay for the card once. Standard, Business and Signature work for you every day: the page, hosting, messages, analytics, changes and support, so they are paid monthly per card.

Can I change the content?+

Yes, whenever you want. Tell us the change, we make it, and the card stays the same.

What if I stop paying?+

With Standard, Business and Signature, the card stops opening your page. The one-time fee covers the card and the setup. Basic has no monthly subscription.

How long does setup take?+

Usually a few days from when we agree. For more complex systems we agree on a timeline up front.
