Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to reply to you and to make the cards work. We do not sell data, show ads or track you with cookies.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of personal data for tiptapreviews.com is TipTap, Lario Vekić, Osijek, Croatia. For any privacy question, write to tiptapbus@gmail.com.

2. Visiting the site

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics tools or ads. Fonts and other files of this site are served from our own domain, over a secure connection (HTTPS).

Our hosting provider, Vercel, logs technical request data such as IP address, time and browser type for security and to keep the service running. Vercel keeps these logs briefly, and we do not use them to track visitors. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Venue pages may store technical settings in your browser, such as the chosen language.

3. Enquiries by form or e-mail

When you send us an enquiry, we receive what you enter: your name, venue or company, mobile or e-mail, the topics you are interested in and your message.

We use it only to reply and prepare an offer. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The form sends the data to our e-mail through the FormSubmit service.

We keep enquiries while we are in contact and for at most two years after the last contact. If you become a client, we keep the data for the duration of our cooperation and as long as the law requires for business records.

4. Cards and venue pages

When a guest taps a TipTap card or scans its QR code, our system redirects them to that venue’s page. This creates the technical logs described in section 2 and, where a venue uses analytics, a count of taps and clicks, with no data about guests.

  • Messages, complaints and orders (for example a gift voucher) that a guest sends from a venue page are sent by e-mail through FormSubmit to the venue, or to us where we receive messages for the venue. They are not stored on the page itself. Messages we receive for a venue are forwarded to it and kept for no longer than 12 months.
  • Anonymous messages require no name or login. Leaving contact details for a reply is optional.
  • Staff and vehicle ratings and work schedules are kept for the venue. Guests give ratings voluntarily and the ratings contain no data about them.
  • Google, Instagram, Facebook and maps: when you tap these buttons, you move to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply. Some venue pages may also load content from other services, such as a weather forecast.

For the data guests send to a venue, and for data about the venue’s staff, the venue is the controller and TipTap processes it on the venue’s behalf as a processor.

5. Who receives data

We do not sell data or share it for advertising. We only use these services:

  • Vercel (USA): website hosting and card redirects.
  • FormSubmit: sending forms by e-mail.
  • Google: our e-mail (Gmail) and the sheet that says where each card leads. The sheet contains no guest data.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to object and to data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

For any of this, write to tiptapbus@gmail.com. We reply within one month at the latest. If you believe we process data unlawfully, you can complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (azop.hr).

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
